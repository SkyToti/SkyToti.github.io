## What it is

A travel agency's website. It includes a page for the ads, an admin panel and a private itinerary page for each traveler.

## Who it's for

The agency owner, who puts the trips together and shares them. And each traveler, who sees their itinerary at their own address.

## How it works

1. A Facebook or Instagram ad leads to the agency's page.
2. The owner logs in to the admin panel.
3. There she builds a trip's itinerary.
4. Each traveler gets a link to their private page.

## My role

I built the site from start to finish, in July and August 2026. I delivered it with a maintenance manual so the agency doesn't depend on me.

## The proof

The site is live at viajaconkarla.com.mx. It runs without a server of its own: it publishes itself every time it changes. The itinerary page in the screenshots is the real one, opened with a sample trip.

## Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Supabase and GitHub Pages.
