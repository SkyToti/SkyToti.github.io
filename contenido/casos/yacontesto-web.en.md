## What it is

The YaContesto page. It explains how the AI receptionist works through an animated story, with a character and their office drawn with code.

## Who it's for

Dental clinic owners deciding whether to ask for a demo on WhatsApp. It is designed for mobile first.

## How it works

1. As you scroll down, the story starts.
2. A message from a patient arrives on the clinic's WhatsApp.
3. The assistant replies and checks the calendar.
4. The appointment lands on the clinic's calendar.
5. At the end, the page invites you to ask for a demo on WhatsApp.

## My role

I directed the AI that drew and animated the story. I set what the page promises and what it doesn't: only what the assistant does today. I also handle sales: prospecting, proposals, a trial contract and on-site installation.

## The proof

The site is only published if every automated check passes. It scores 100 on Lighthouse, Google's measurement tool, on mobile. You can open it and check for yourself.

## Technology

Next.js 16, React 19, GSAP ScrollTrigger, SVG, Tailwind 4 and GitHub Actions.
